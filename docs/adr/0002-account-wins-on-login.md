# Account wins over Guest on log-in

One Play per Player per Game Day, whether Guest or Account. Creating an Account is the Guest becoming that Account, so their Plays are kept. Logging into an existing Account is switching Player: the Account’s Plays always replace the Guest’s. We do not merge boards or Statistics. Simpler and safer than stitching two histories; the cost is throwing away that device’s Guest guesses, even if the Account has no Play for today.
